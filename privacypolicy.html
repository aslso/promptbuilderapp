Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 16, 2025

Vantra ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication data through secure third-party providers (Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In).
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the app, including prompts you create and preferences you set.
  • Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and advertising purposes.
  • Generated Content: AI-generated images created within the app are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription and in-app purchase events are processed by Apple/Google and RevenueCat. We may receive non-sensitive entitlement/status information to enable features.
  • Usage (Credit) Data: Your remaining/used usage units (credits) may be stored locally on your device to enforce usage limits and display your balance.

Photo and Image Data (Face Data)

Important: This section explains how we handle photos you may upload when using our Photo Remix feature.

Collection

When you use our Photo Remix feature, you may voluntarily upload photos that could contain faces or other personal imagery. This is entirely optional - the core functionality of Vantra works with text-based AI prompts without requiring any photo uploads.

Processing

Uploaded photos are sent to Google's Gemini API for AI-powered image processing. The AI uses your photo as a reference to generate a new, modified image based on your text instructions. We do not perform any facial recognition, biometric analysis, or identity verification.

Storage

We do not store your uploaded photos on our servers. Photos are held temporarily in your device's memory during the active processing session only. Once processing is complete or you close the app, the original uploaded photo is not retained by us.

Third-Party Sharing

Your uploaded photos are transmitted to Google (Gemini API) solely for the purpose of AI image generation. No other third parties receive your photos. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for their data handling practices.

Retention

We do not retain uploaded photos after your session ends. We do not maintain any database or backup of user-uploaded photos. Google's API processes images in real-time, and their retention policies apply to their service.

Your Control

You have full control over what photos you upload. Since we do not store your photos on our servers, there is no photo data to delete from our systems. Generated images are stored only on your device and can be deleted by you at any time.

AI-Generated Content

Vantra uses Google's Gemini AI to generate images based on your text prompts. When you generate an image:

  • Your text prompt is sent to Google's Gemini API
  • The generated image is returned to your device
  • Generated images are stored locally on your device only
  • We do not store your prompts or generated images on our servers

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain our service
  • Save your preferences and settings locally
  • Improve and personalize your experience
  • Process subscription payments
  • Enforce usage limits and show your usage balance
  • Display relevant advertisements (for free users)

Advertising

For users on the free tier, we display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifier
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. Pro subscribers do not see advertisements.

Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely using Firebase, a Google Cloud service. We implement appropriate security measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS) to protect your personal information. Generated images and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase: Authentication and basic account data storage
  • Google Gemini API: AI image generation
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • Google AdMob: Advertising (free tier only)
  • Apple/Google: Payment processing for subscriptions

Each of these services has their own privacy policy governing their use of data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Delete locally stored images and preferences from your device

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days. Locally stored data on your device remains under your control.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
